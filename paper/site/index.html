The Start of the Window: RoPE's Critical Dimension Predicts the Base Frequency Needed for 128K–256K Context Extension

Birger Moëll

AI Sweden · November 2026 · Version 1.0

PDF Code, raw trials & scripts Models Cite

Abstract

When a language model pretrained at 4K tokens is extended to 128K by raising the RoPE base frequency θ and continuing pretraining, it can retrieve facts from the middle and end of the window yet fail almost completely on facts placed at its very start. We study this depth-0 failure in a 9B-parameter multilingual OpenEuroLLM model extended to 128K and 256K tokens, using a forced-choice needle-in-a-haystack test in up to 15 languages and 20–90 trials per cell. Three results stand out. First, data does not fix it: two length-biased data mixes leave depth-0 accuracy at 64K at 0/20 and 1/20, below the 25% chance level, because the model is drawn to distractors seen recently. Second, θ does: in a controlled sweep from one 64K checkpoint, with identical data and 294M tokens per arm, depth-0 accuracy at 128K rises from 20% (θ = 8M) to 80% (16M) to 100% (32M). Third, the critical-dimension theory of RoPE extrapolation (Liu et al., 2024) predicts these thresholds without fitting: with a 4K pretraining window and base 10⁵, only the first 74 of 128 rotary dimensions complete a rotation in pretraining, giving a critical base of 8.9M at 64K, 29.6M at 128K and 98.2M at 256K. Pooled over all runs, retrieval is 97% (1954/2005) accurate when the needle lies within the critical period and 7% (8/120) beyond twice it. The theory implies that the critical base grows by a factor of 3.32 per doubling of context, not 2, so the common “double θ per octave” heuristic under-provisions 512K and 1M models by 2.5× and 4.2×. A more-pretrained 1T-token base, extended with an identical recipe, fixes depth-0 but loses accuracy in the middle of the window (94% versus 100% of mid-window trials correct), which a 1.26× token budget did not repair. We release the models, raw trials, launch scripts and analysis code.

Key results

  • Far-position (depth-0) retrieval failure after context extension did not respond to two length-biased data mixes; failing models score below chance (0/20, 1/20 at 64K).
  • Controlled θ sweep at 128K, same checkpoint and data: depth-0 accuracy 20% (8M) → 80% (16M) → 100% (32M).
  • RoPE critical-dimension theory, computed from the pretraining configuration alone, predicts the threshold: 8.9M at 64K, 29.6M at 128K, 98M at 256K.
  • Across 2,445 long-context trials, retrieval is 97% accurate within the critical period and 7% beyond twice it.
  • The critical base grows ×3.3 per context doubling, not ×2: the doubling rule under-provisions 512K and 1M by 2.5× and 4.2×.
  • A 1T-token base with the identical recipe fixes depth-0 but keeps a lost-in-the-middle dip (94% vs 100%).
